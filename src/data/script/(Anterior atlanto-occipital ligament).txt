

(ANTERIOR ATLANTO-OCCIPITAL LIGAMENT)


The anterior atlanto-dental ligament is an odd anterior ligament of the atlanto-occipital joint.

It is 2-3 cm wide and thick in its middle part.


It attaches above to the basilar part of the occipital bone.

Its outer deep fibers merge with the capsule of the atlanto-occipital joint.
